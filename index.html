Our story

A workshop in the Medina, not a lab.

Jamal Marrakech began in a single room off a quiet derb in the old city, where our founder mixed argan oil the way her mother had — by hand, by feel, in small batches meant for neighbors before they were meant for customers.

Every jar is still filled in that same workshop today. We source cold-pressed argan from women's cooperatives in the Souss valley, wild-harvested prickly pear seed, and rose water steam-distilled in the Dades Valley — nothing synthetic, nothing rushed.
