Where should I begin?

Explore the three-step ritual above, or start with one product that fits your current routine. Check the product’s full label and instructions before use.

Can I save products for later?

Yes. Tap the heart on a product card, then use the Saved filter or the heart in the header. Saved items stay in this browser when local storage is available.

Can I place an order on this preview?

You can explore products, choose sizes, and try the shopping bag. This is a storefront demo: checkout, payments, stock, and delivery are not connected yet. No order is placed.

Are these the final product photos and sizes?

The coordinated packaging images and size choices are design placeholders. Replace them with your final photography, confirmed sizes, ingredient lists, and prices before publishing the shop.
